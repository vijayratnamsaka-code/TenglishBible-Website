Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

TenglishBible respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the TenglishBible Android application handles user information.

1. No Personal Data Collection

TenglishBible is a fully offline Bible application. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information from users.

2. No Account Required

TenglishBible does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or password.

3. Offline Application

The Bible content and core functionality of TenglishBible are available offline. The app does not require an internet connection to read the Bible.

4. No Tracking or Analytics

TenglishBible does not use third-party analytics, tracking technologies, or advertising services to monitor user activity.

5. No Advertising

TenglishBible does not display third-party advertisements.

6. Data Sharing

Since TenglishBible does not collect personal information, there is no personal data to sell, share, or disclose to third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

TenglishBible does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The application can be used without providing personal information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app's functionality or privacy practices change, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly. Any updated version will be published on this website.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or TenglishBible, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the official website.
